MY STORY

Curiosity is where it starts.

I'm Lizalise Hlangani, an ICT student with a background in Application Development and a growing interest in networking, IT support, and full-stack development.

My journey has been shaped by my experiences, education, determination, and curiosity about technology. I enjoy understanding how things work, solving problems, and turning what I learn into practical solutions.

My Journey

I was born in Klerksdorp, North West, and later moved to the Eastern Cape. As life took me through different places and experiences, I learned the importance of adapting, persevering, and continuing to work toward my goals.

I attended Suleman Patel Secondary School and completed my matric in 2022. My educational journey eventually brought me to Durban University of Technology, where I am studying towards a Diploma in Information and Communication Technology, specialising in Application Development.

During my ICT studies, I developed a deeper appreciation for technology. What began as learning about computers and software grew into a genuine interest in how applications are designed, how databases manage information, and how networks connect devices and systems.

I fell in love with ICT because it combines creativity, logical thinking, and problem-solving. I enjoy the process of learning something new, experimenting with it, and seeing how it works in practice.

My journey has also involved managing financial responsibilities while continuing my education and developing my technical abilities. These experiences have taught me persistence, independence, and the value of working toward long-term goals.

Today, I am developing myself as a versatile developer and ICT practitioner. I am interested in both front-end and back-end development, alongside networking and hands-on IT support. I want to understand technology from different perspectives and build skills that allow me to contribute to real-world solutions.
